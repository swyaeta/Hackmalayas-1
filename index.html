What is Hackmalayas

Hackmalayas is a flagship Hack Club event happening in the Himalayas. Build creative games or software projects for 60 hours and you can earn an invite to our coding retreat!

Your food and accommodation will be covered — any work you do after getting your ticket counts toward your flight stipend or anything else you might want to purchase from our shop. If you can't make it to the event, use your hours to buy yourself gifts from the shop!

Steps to attend the irl event

  1. 1 Get an idea for a game or software project!
  2. 2 Build one project or multiple for up to 60 hours
  3. 3 Log your hours on Hackatime
  4. 4 Ship your project!
  5. 5 Buy a ticket and become a Hackmalayas attendee!

Rules

  • • Every project must be open source.
  • • All work must be tracked with Hackatime.
  • • AI may be used only for debugging or very minor tasks only 30%.
  • • Games and software only. Hardware projects are not accepted for Hackmalayas.

The Currency

Track your hours using Hackatime to earn wools. Normal projects get 4 wools per hour, while snow and nature-themed projects get 5 wools per hour!

The shop will have iPad grants, laptop grants, digicams to click the Himalayas, flight stipends, Hack Club stickers and swag, plus all the other stuff people suggest!
